Common questions from agency directors

We've heard your concerns

Straight answers to what directors ask us most.

"Are there cameras involved?"

Absolutely not. Our smart devices are entirely passive and unobtrusive your clients simply live their normal lives without any cameras, recording devices, or anything that feels invasive. Privacy and dignity are at the core of how we operate.

"What if a sensor has an issue?"

We handle all support directly with the family. Your staff never fields a tech question — that's entirely our responsibility.

"Does this create liability for us?"

No. We make no health claims. Bekway is a family notification service, not a clinical tool. Your agency simply refers.

"What does my team do day to day?"

Nothing. Once you refer a client, we manage the entire relationship. Zero ongoing work for your care team.

"What sensors do you use?"

We use enterprise-grade wireless sensors specifically selected for reliability, long battery life, and performance in older homes with weak WiFi so your clients never experience technical disruptions.
