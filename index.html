How it works

It learns their normal, so it can spot what isn't

Bekway isn't just a set of sensors, it's a running picture of a normal day, so an unusual one stands out immediately.

01

Learns the rhythm

Over the first couple of weeks, Bekway quietly learns their day: when they usually wake up, when the kitchen gets used, when the front door opens and closes.

02

Watches for change

Every day after that, sensors compare what's happening now against that normal pattern, not a fixed rule, but their actual routine.

03

Flags what's unusual

A stretch of no motion in a normally active room, a door that never opened at the usual time, a morning that looks nothing like the rest, that's what gets flagged.

04

Alerts you instantly

You get a real time alert the moment something looks off, wherever you are, so you can decide whether to call, check in, or just keep watching.

Common questions

Good to know

Straight answers to what families ask us most.

Are there cameras involved?

No. Our system is entirely passive, motion sensors and door sensors only. No cameras, no recording, nothing invasive. Privacy and dignity are at the core of everything we do.

What if a sensor has an issue?

We handle all support directly with you. You'll never need to troubleshoot anything on your own.

Is this a medical device?

No. Bekway makes no health claims. It's a family notification service, not a clinical or diagnostic tool, so it works alongside any care your parent already receives.

Will my parent or loved one need to learn an app?

No app or device is required on their end. The sensors work quietly in the background, and only the family members monitoring from afar use the Bekway app.

What sensors do you use?

Enterprise grade wireless sensors built for reliability and long battery life, including in older homes with weak WiFi. You won't experience technical disruptions.
