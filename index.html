How it works

No software to install and no long implementation. You send a small sample and we show you what we find.

  1. Step 1

    Send sanitized sample data

    Share a small batch of notes, claims or policies with patient identifiers removed. We tell you exactly what to include.

  2. Step 2

    Review your vulnerability report

    You get a plain-language report of what we found, what it is likely costing you, and what to fix first. It’s yours to keep, whether or not we work together.

  3. Step 3

    We automate and manage the fix

    If you want help, we put the service in place and run it. Your team gets time back instead of another system to learn.
