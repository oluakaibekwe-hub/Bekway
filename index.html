Common questions from agency directors

We've heard your concerns

Straight answers to what directors ask us most.

"Are there cameras involved?"

Absolutely not. Our system is entirely passive — motion sensors and door sensors only. No cameras, no recording, nothing invasive. Privacy and dignity are at the core of everything we do.

"What if a sensor has an issue?"

We handle all support directly with the family. Your staff never fields a tech question — that's entirely our responsibility.

"Does this create liability for us?"

No. We make no health claims. Bekway is a family notification service, not a clinical tool. Your agency simply refers — nothing more.

"What does my team do day to day?"

Almost nothing. Your team's only role is the referral conversation. We handle consultation, installation, family onboarding, and all ongoing support from there.

"What sensors do you use?"

Enterprise-grade wireless sensors built for reliability and long battery life — including in older homes with weak WiFi. Your clients will never experience technical disruptions.
