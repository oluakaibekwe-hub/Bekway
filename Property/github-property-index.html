FAQ

Straight answers to what owners ask us most

Do I need to change my WiFi or add new equipment?

No. Sensors run on their own low-power wireless network through a small hub connected to your existing internet or a cellular connection.

Why does pricing vary by sensor point?

A studio apartment and a single-family house don't carry the same risk — a house typically has more bathrooms, a garage, or a detached water heater. Pricing per point keeps things fair without a fully custom quote for every property.

What if a sensor stops working?

We monitor sensor health remotely and handle replacement at no charge under your plan.

Does this replace my existing alarm system?

No — it complements it. Most alarm systems don't watch for leaks, access, or equipment power draw at all.

Is there a long-term contract?

No. Month-to-month, cancel with 30 days' notice.
