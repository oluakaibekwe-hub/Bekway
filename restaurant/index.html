FAQ

Straight answers to what owners ask us most

Do I need to change my WiFi or add new equipment?

No. Sensors run on their own low-power wireless network through a small hub connected to your existing internet or a cellular connection.

What if a sensor stops working?

We monitor sensor health remotely and handle replacement at no charge under your plan.

Will this help with health inspections?

Yes — continuous logs are available on request, giving you a documented temperature history for any unit.

Is there a long-term contract?

No. Month-to-month, cancel with 30 days' notice.
